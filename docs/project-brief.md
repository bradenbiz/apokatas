# Apokatas project brief

Recorded from the user's initial idea on 2026-09-12. This is an idea inventory, not an approved specification or a set of implemented capabilities.

## Purpose

Create an open-source, gold-backed monetary system to bring gold and silver back as money and support what the user calls sound money. Other metals, specifically copper and platinum, were mentioned as possible additions. The precise meaning of open and the required monetary guarantees are still being discussed.

## Inspirations

The user named e-gold, Goldmoney, Kinesis, Tether Gold, Pax Gold, and "t-gold by Schiff Gold" as predecessors or competitors. The intention is to improve on their deficiencies and failure points; the user's priority failure points have not yet been specified.

The [preliminary predecessor notes](research/predecessors.md) preserve early factual findings. They are not a completed competitive assessment or endorsements.

## Candidate features

1. Spot markets with simple trade execution.
2. Quick and easy fiat deposits and withdrawals.
3. Simple, timely, and affordable physical redemption.
4. Regular, frequent, and trustworthy audits of metal and fiat.
5. Free pooled storage and automated, affordable segregated storage.
6. Physical and digital debit cards.
7. Clean and intuitive interfaces.
8. Quality documentation and issue remediation.
9. Automated tax reporting.
10. Possible yields for stakeholders or token holders. The user is explicitly unsure about this and whether Kinesis's KVT model has been a good idea.

These are candidate capabilities, not commitments for the first release. The costs of free storage, the source of any yield, and the viability of the overall economics remain open. Mentioning tokens does not select a blockchain or token architecture.

## Initial operating preference

The user considers vertical integration unlikely to be practical initially and prefers exploring service providers for banking, vaulting, cards, and audits as a possible route to an MVP. This is a starting hypothesis, not a verified provider plan.

Rush, BullionVault, Kitco Digital Metals, and nFusion were subsequently supplied as [unverified provider leads](research/provider-leads.md).

## Planning status

The assistant proposed a decision-complete design for a first viable pilot as the planning destination. The user has not accepted or changed that proposal. The [opening questions](planning/open-questions.md) must be answered before the map's destination and initial decision issues can be settled.
