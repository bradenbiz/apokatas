## E-gold

The US Department of Justice's July 21, 2008 announcement records guilty pleas by E-Gold and Gold & Silver Reserve concerning money-laundering conspiracy and operating an unlicensed money-transmitting business. The agreement included identification, reporting, supervision, and bullion-backing audit requirements.

This source does not establish missing reserves as the cause of failure, nor does it establish the complete chronology of the eventual shutdown.

Source: [DOJ plea announcement](https://www.justice.gov/archive/opa/pr/2008/July/08-crm-635.html).

**Planning implication proposed by the assistant:** Investigate operating viability alongside metal backing. This has not yet been translated into an Apokatas design decision.

## Pax Gold and Tether Gold

The PAXG terms examined described fractional beneficial ownership of gold held in approved vaults. Direct physical redemption required a verified Paxos account and at least 430 PAXG plus fees per bar, with excess returned after the actual bar weight was determined. The terms also described freeze and upgrade powers.

The Tether Gold FAQ examined described verified issuer access and whole-bar redemption, advised depositing 430 XAUT, and described physical delivery within Switzerland, with a 0.25% charge plus delivery costs.

These were published terms, not tested redemption experiences or evidence of current availability. They show why fractional balances and practical small-quantity physical redemption should be investigated separately.

Sources: [PAXG terms](https://www.paxos.com/terms-and-conditions/pax-gold-terms-conditions), [Tether Gold FAQ](https://gold.tether.to/faq).

## Kinesis and KVT

Kinesis's KVT yield page examined described a collective allocation of 20% of eligible global transaction fees to KVT holders, paid proportionately in monthly KAU/KAG distributions. It described returns as variable with activity and not guaranteed. The white-paper summary distinguished KVT from the gold- and silver-backed currencies.

This establishes the published mechanism examined, not whether KVT has been commercially successful or would be appropriate for Apokatas. The user's question about whether this was a good design remains open.

Sources: [KVT yield page](https://kinesis.money/kvt-yield/), [Kinesis white-paper summary](https://kinesis.money/resources/kinesis-whitepaper-summary-en.pdf).

## SchiffGold and TGold

The user's "t-gold by Schiff Gold" reference appears to correspond to TGold. A SchiffGold interview dated February 2, 2026 described TGold.com as offering gold and silver purchase and storage, with token issuance forthcoming. When checked on 2026-09-12, TGold.com redirected to SchiffGold Trade, which marketed allocated gold and silver accounts and bank transfers.

These sources did not establish that token issuance had launched. Identity and current capabilities should be checked again before comparing products.

Sources: [SchiffGold interview](https://www.schiffgold.com/interviews/schiff-on-coinage-gold-is-heading-to-7000), [SchiffGold Trade page](https://www.schiffgold.com/services/start-trading).
