## Questions for later verification

These are proposed research checks, not already-created Wayfinder issues:

- Is the offering market data, trading-account automation, embedded trading and custody, vault logistics, or a combination?
- Does it support an independent platform with its own end customers and transfers between them?
- Who owns the metal, who is the customer of record, and who bears the custody and operating obligations?
- What locations, metals, account structures, audits, insurance, and pooled or segregated storage options are supported?
- What physical redemption quantities, destinations, lead times, and total charges apply?
- What are the integration, onboarding, geography, commercial minimum, and recurring-cost requirements?
- What records and rights survive a provider termination or failure, and how could customers or holdings move elsewhere?
